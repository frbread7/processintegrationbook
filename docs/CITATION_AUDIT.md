# Citation and claim audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-12  
**Scope:** Every scholarly source in `docs/REFERENCES.md` and every source-specific technical claim in the English/Korean chapters, especially the thermal-history model and the public high-k/metal-gate case.  
**Disposition:** Claim support is documented for this candidate, with original parameter provenance explicitly unresolved. Targeted technical, documentation/release, and security reviews found no actionable findings at commit `7b3cf641c619f9a7d65344ef490ff80e0dcbdaad`; the original Sze/Scotten page or table remains unverified. This audit is not professional certification, process calibration, or silicon validation.

## Method and decision rules

- Checked bibliographic metadata against publisher DOI records where available; used university/library indexes for cataloged books and the Fair article record.
- Read publisher abstracts and publicly accessible full text for claims. For the Mistry worked case, checked the paper's Figure 5 and nearby prose on pp. 247–248 against the chapter description.
- Recorded the exact scope of each source. A paper supports only its reported platform, material system, conditions, or method; it does not establish a universal recipe or validate this book's models.
- Kept illustrative scenarios, synthetic values, and engineering inferences separate from reported source claims.
- DOI metadata and access status were checked on the sources linked below. A reference whose full text is paywalled or mirrored is not presented as an independent experiment performed by this project.

## Chapter-level claim map

- **Chapters 1–3:** Mistry supports only features of its reported 45 nm platform; Yeo supports general metal-gate material/interface/integration context. Methodological recommendations are this book's decision framework, not claimed quotations or empirical results from those sources.
- **Chapter 4:** Aitken is further reading for predictive-technology context. The substrate/isolation scenario is fictional and is not attributed to Aitken.
- **Chapter 5:** Mistry and Yeo provide historical platform and review context for gate-stack choices. No universal gate-first/gate-last outcome is asserted.
- **Chapter 6 and thermal lab:** Esser reports the Arrhenius pair in an illustrative calculation; Mostafa and Medraj also print the equation and units. Esser cites Sze (1983), while the review cites Scotten (2000); neither historical source's original page/table has been independently checked, so provenance is unresolved. Fair supports a condition-limited concentration caution; Bracht and Sadigh support only the stated experimental and mechanism context. Equations are evaluated and independently reimplemented in Python; that is an analytical cross-check, not validation against silicon.
- **Chapters 7–9:** Mistry supports only the cited 193 nm dry patterning, trench-contact/local-routing, and nine-Cu-layer features of its historical platform. Links to specialist books identify complementary scope; they do not imply those books authored this work.
- **Chapters 10–12:** The variation example is instructional synthesis. Aitken supports the bounded predictive-technology/DTCO framing in chapter 11 and the further-reading context in chapter 12, not the fictional data or its proposed tests.
- **Chapter 13:** The public sequence and stated workfunction/thermal-budget rationale are traced to Mistry Fig. 5 and nearby text on pp. 247–248. Proposed transfer conditions and measurements are explicitly engineering inference; Yeo supplies broader review context only.

No third-party paper figures, copied paragraphs, source code, or datasets are included. All example datasets/scenarios in this book are labeled as synthetic or illustrative. This audit does not claim independent confirmation of reported process performance, recipe suitability, simulator-to-silicon agreement, or fabrication results.
