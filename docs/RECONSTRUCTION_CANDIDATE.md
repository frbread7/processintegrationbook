## Laboratory and evidence disposition

- **Thermal history:** zero to 30 holds are allowed. Zero holds display the initial reflected profile. The result includes component width, actual mode, right-side background crossing when present, an adaptive plotted peak, and a crossing marker. `docs/MODELS_AND_VALIDATION.md` gives the equation, units, initial/boundary conditions, assumptions, invalid regimes, and independent analytical/numerical reference checks.
- **Dependency explorer:** each of three fixed focus cases exposes authored directed source-to-target relations, relation type (precedence, compatibility, or inference), evidence status, and an unresolved question. It teaches interface review; it cannot validate a process recipe.
- **Integration review:** bounded qualitative choices remain evidence-led and ordinal, without fabricated probabilities or automated fault attribution.
- **Sources and rights:** [RECONSTRUCTION_CLAIM_REGISTER.md](RECONSTRUCTION_CLAIM_REGISTER.md) separates inspected full text/passage, abstract-only evidence, original derivations, and synthetic cases. New diagrams and prose are original; no third-party figures or proprietary process data were adapted. This is a provenance record, not legal certification.
