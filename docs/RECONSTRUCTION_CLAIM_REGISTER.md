## Quantitative model validation boundary

`thermal-model.js` is a deterministic synthetic diffusion teaching model. Its input constraints are σ₀ 0.5–100 nm, lobe center 0–500 nm, Q 10¹¹–10¹⁵ cm⁻², NB 10¹⁴–10¹⁹ cm⁻³, and at most 30 constant-temperature holds (850–1100 °C, 0.1–3600 s each). Zero holds are mathematically allowed for an initial-profile check. Initial condition is the reflected Gaussian at σ₀; boundary condition is zero concentration gradient at x=0; the far field tends to zero modeled dopant concentration. The background is a separate constant threshold, not diffused by this solver. No moving boundary or reaction is included.

The reference case (σ₀=20 nm, Rp=80 nm, Q=2×10¹³ cm⁻², NB=10¹⁶ cm⁻³, 1050 °C for 20 s) evaluates to `D=5.034258593235×10⁻¹⁴ cm²/s`, `σf=24.5228534989 nm`, mode approximately 80 nm, peak approximately `3.2536366997×10¹⁸ cm⁻³`, and descending crossing `163.413350077 nm`. The independently integrated dose in `tests/test_thermal_reference.py` includes the required nm→cm factor `10⁻⁷`; boundary derivative and concentration root checks are separate from implementation calls. A valid extreme 30×3600 s at 1100 °C gives σf approximately 1814.65 nm and a descending crossing near 8642.95 nm for the stated deep-case profile; the chart must include the crossing rather than clip at 1000 nm. Narrow valid peaks are sampled at the actual mode in addition to a coarse axis grid. These are analytical and numerical checks only, not comparison with implant metrology or silicon data.
