참고 문헌 및 범위 연결

공개 자료는 특정 배경 설명을 위해 인용합니다. 아래 독립 교재는 별도 프로젝트이며 링크는 자료를 복제하거나 보증한다는 뜻이 아닙니다.

공개 논문 및 기술 배경

  1. Fair (1975) — Boron diffusion: concentration, orientation, background, and profile estimation. DOI / 출판사.
  2. Bracht et al. (2007) — Silicon diffusion measurements and native point-defect contributions. DOI / 출판사.
  3. Sadigh et al. (1999) — Atomistic and kinetic Monte Carlo study of boron diffusion mechanism. DOI / 출판사.
  4. Yeo (2004) — Metal-gate material selection and process-integration issues. DOI / 출판사.
  5. Aitken et al. (2016) — Predictive technology and design-technology co-optimization. DOI / 출판사.
  6. Mistry et al. (IEDM 2007) — Publicly reported 45 nm platform feature list; source-limited case in chapter 13. DOI / 출판사.
  7. Esser et al. (2004) — Public calculation reproducing the boron Arrhenius pair; cites Sze (1983) as the historical source. DOI / 출판사.
  8. Mostafa and Medraj (2017) — Review reproducing the intrinsic boron diffusivity equation and units; cites Scotten (2000), whose source page was not independently checked. DOI / 원문.
  9. Sze, ed. (1983) — VLSI Technology; historical source cited by Esser et al., without an independently verified page locator. 도서관 서지 기록.
  10. Sze and Ng (2007) — General semiconductor-device reference; not used as the source for the selected Arrhenius pair. 출판사 서지 기록.
  11. Ivrii, Partial Differential Equations — Neumann half-line heat-equation image construction; mathematical boundary model, not a physical implant-surface claim. 토론토 대학교 교재.
  12. NIST CODATA (2018) — Energy-equivalent Boltzmann constant used in the thermal model. NIST 상수 표.
  13. Li et al. — Nanowire contact/path extraction and Kelvin method context; not validation of the synthetic planar-sheet example. NIST 공개 논문.
  14. Reeves and Harrison (1982) — TLM contact-end and under-contact sheet-resistance caveat; metadata and abstract inspected. DOI / 초록.
  15. Aharoni (1976) — Silicon epitaxial stacking-fault origin alternatives; abstract inspected. DOI / 초록.
  16. Minowa et al. (2000) — Optical-defect and TEM observations in reported silicon wafers; abstract inspected. DOI / 초록.
  17. IUCr Online Dictionary of Crystallography — Stacking-fault definition as a planar defect. 용어 사전.
  18. Fiory et al. (2002) — Study distinguishing chemical profile, electrical activation, and leakage evidence; not calibration of this lab. DOI / 출판사.
  19. Tian et al. (1998) — Implant-damage and transient-enhanced-diffusion model context; abstract inspected. DOI / 초록.
