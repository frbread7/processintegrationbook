Thermal history and a reflected Gaussian profile estimate

Accumulate a one-dimensional diffusion integral across constant-temperature holds. The model reflects an idealized Gaussian implant lobe at a no-flux surface, preserving the stated dose in silicon. Rp is the lobe center near the surface. This is not implant boundary chemistry, an activated profile, a measured junction depth, or a process recipe.

Method background: Neumann image solution for a reflecting half-line; MIT OpenCourseWare diffusion model.
